# WissensWerk
Understanding - Developing - Documenting 

_WissensWerk is a documented journey through the development of a modern Joomla template—from the first architectural decision to the finished implementation._

## What is WissensWerk?
WissensWerk is a long-term learning and development project.

The focus is not just on developing a Joomla template, but above all on transparently documenting the entire development process – from the initial idea and architectural decisions to practical solutions for real-world problems.

This repository combines source code, documentation, and practical experience into a shared knowledge base.

## Goal
The goal is not to develop a finished template as quickly as possible.
Rather, the aim is to create a project that transparently documents the entire development process and can serve as a foundation for future projects.
Every major decision is justified.
Every technical challenge is documented.
Every solution is practically verified.

## Core Principles
- Architecture over implementation
- Semantics over design
- Clarity over complexity
- Documentation is part of development
- Decisions are justified
- Experiences are recorded
- Practically tested solutions take precedence over theoretical assumptions

## AI Assistance

Artificial intelligence is used throughout the development process to support research, architectural discussions, documentation, source code development, and the analysis and evaluation of technical solutions.

As this project is also a learning journey for its author, AI serves as a sparring partner to deepen the understanding of technical concepts, compare different approaches, and continuously build new knowledge. All proposed solutions are carefully reviewed, practically tested, and critically evaluated before they become part of the project or its documentation.

Within this project, AI is not a substitute for understanding or hands-on experimentation. Instead, it supports the development process by helping to build knowledge, make well-informed decisions, and document results in a clear and traceable manner.

This project aims to demonstrate that sustainable learning and reliable solutions cannot be achieved through AI alone. They are primarily the result of experimentation, critical thinking, and a genuine willingness to understand how things work. WissensWerk encourages the use of AI not merely as a tool for obtaining quick answers, but as a means of becoming actively involved, learning new skills, and continuously developing one's own abilities.

Responsibility for the project's architecture, source code, documentation, and the professional evaluation and quality of all results rests solely with the author of this project
